# Alerts center

The Alerts center is the one place to see every alert — price alerts and Pine Script alerts
alike.

## What you'll find

Alerts are grouped by state:

- **Active** — currently armed and waiting.
- **Triggered** — fired recently.
- **Paused** — turned off but kept for later.
- **History** — everything that has fired.

## Search and filter

Search and filter the list to zero in on a specific symbol or alert type.

## Managing alerts

From the Alerts center you can:

- create a new alert
- pause or resume an alert
- review what triggered and when

Pine Script alerts triggered by a script's `alert()` call appear here too, alongside price
alerts.
