# Paper trading

Paper trading lets you practice with simulated money. It's local and persistent, and paper
orders **never** route to a live exchange.

## Order types

The order ticket supports:

- **Market** — fill at the current price.
- **Limit** — fill only at your price or better.
- **Stop** — trigger into an order when price reaches a level.

## Position tracking

DegenView tracks your net position with weighted-average cost, the same accounting used by
[Portfolio](/portfolio/overview). Open positions and resting orders are marked directly on the
chart, so you can see where you stand as you watch price.

## Why use it

- Test an idea before risking anything.
- Learn the order flow without a live account.
- See how a strategy would have behaved over time.
