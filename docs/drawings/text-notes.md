# Text, notes, images & posts

Use these tools to label a chart, keep notes next to the price, add a table or a picture, and sketch with brushes, arrows and shapes. For placing, selecting and undoing, see the [overview](/drawings/overview).

## Text and notes tile

| Section | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Text and notes | **Text**, **Note**, **Price note**, **Pin**, **Table**, **Callout**, **Comment**, **Price label**, **Signpost**, **Flag mark** |
| Content | **Image**, **Post** |

### Type on the chart

**Text**, **Comment**, **Note**, **Callout** and **Signpost** open an editor as soon as you place them.

- Return keeps the text. Shift+Return adds a line.
- Esc drops the edit and leaves no drawing.
- Other drawings show a faint **Add text** when selected.
- Text is limited to 500 characters, or 2,000 for **Pin**, **Note** and **Callout**.

![Notes on made-up sample candles](/images/drawings/notes.png)

### Pins

A **Pin** marks a spot on the chart. Click it to open its note, and press Esc or click elsewhere to close it. Which pins are open is not saved.

### Tables

A **Table** holds up to 30 rows and 12 columns, with 500 characters per cell. Click a cell to edit it. Tab, Shift+Tab and the arrow keys move between cells. Right-click for **Add row**, **Add column**, **Delete row** and **Delete column**, and **Pin to screen** or **Pin to chart**.

![A table on made-up sample candles](/images/drawings/table.png)

### Images

1. Pick **Image**. A sheet opens.
2. Choose a file, use **Paste from clipboard**, or drop a file on the sheet.
3. Place the image on the chart.

Only PNG and JPEG work. DegenView shrinks the picture and strips its metadata, so keep your original. Opacity runs from 5 to 100 %. The right-click menu has **Replace image…** and **Reset aspect ratio**.

::: warning Platform differences
⌘V does not paste an image. Use the paste button. On Windows there is no file picker, so use the drop zone. The clipboard button is macOS only.
:::

### Posts

A **Post** is a link card for an X (Twitter) post. It accepts x.com and twitter.com status links only. The first preview asks for your one-time consent, and then contacts X's servers only. The right-click menu has **Open link**, **Copy link**, **Replace link…** and **Fetch preview…**. The card is anchored to the candle of the post's time.

## Brushes, arrows and shapes

| Section | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Brushes | **Brush**, **Highlighter** |
| Arrows | **Arrow marker**, **Arrow**, **Arrow mark up**, **Arrow mark down** |
| Shapes | **Rectangle**, **Rotated rectangle**, **Path**, **Circle**, **Ellipse**, **Polyline**, **Triangle**, **Arc**, **Curve**, **Double curve** |

Brushes are drawn by pressing, dragging and releasing. **Path** and **Polyline** take a click per vertex. Hold Shift to keep a **Rectangle** or **Ellipse** square. Shapes can carry text, and the magnet does not affect brush strokes.

## Not available

TradingView's "Idea" tool is not built.
