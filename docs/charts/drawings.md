# Drawings & tools

Draw directly on a chart to mark up your analysis. Everything you draw is anchored to time
and price, so it stays in place as the chart scrolls and zooms.

## Drawing tools

- **Trend line** — draw a line between two points to show direction or support/resistance.
- **Fibonacci retracement** — mark retracement levels between a high and a low.
- **Brush** — freehand strokes for quick notes.

## Ruler

The ruler measures a temporary move. Drag it across the chart to read:

- percent change
- price change
- duration
- bar count

## Undo and redo

Drawings support per-window undo and redo:

- Undo: `⌘Z` (macOS) / `Ctrl+Z` (Windows/Linux)
- Redo: `⇧⌘Z` (macOS) / `Ctrl+Shift+Z` (Windows/Linux)

Undo history is per window, so undoing in one chart doesn't affect another.
