# Portfolio: overview and holdings

The Portfolio tab tracks what you own. You record buys, sells and other transactions, and DegenView values them at live prices, so you can see value, profit and allocation in one place.

## Open the Portfolio tab

Click the **Portfolio Tracker** icon at the right end of the chart toolbar. It opens as its own tab. A section bar under the header switches between **Overview**, **Holdings**, **Transactions** and **Statistics**.

![The Overview section with the value chart, allocation and top holdings](/images/portfolio/overview.png)

## Create and manage portfolios

You need at least one portfolio before you can add anything. On a fresh install the page shows a single **Create Portfolio** button.

1. Click **Create Portfolio**, or open the portfolio picker in the header and choose **Create Portfolio…**.
2. Type a name. Names are up to 40 characters and must be unique.
3. Pick a base currency. Transactions in that portfolio are valued in it, and you can still view it in another currency later.
4. Click **Create Portfolio**.

The picker lists **All Portfolios** and each portfolio you made. **Manage Portfolios…** lets you drag to reorder, click a name to rename, duplicate a portfolio with its transactions, or delete it.

::: tip
Pick a specific portfolio before you add or import transactions. With **All Portfolios** selected, a new transaction goes into your first portfolio.
:::

## Overview

The top of the page shows your total value and a small dot for price freshness: green for live, orange for cached or partly missing prices, red for failed. Prices revalue at most once every two seconds.

Below it you get:

- a value chart with ranges **1D**, **1W**, **1M**, **1Y** and **ALL** (it opens on 1M);
- an **Allocation** donut;
- **Top Holdings**, with **View All** to jump to Holdings;
- **Best Performer** and **Worst Performer** tiles.

A wide window uses two columns. A narrow one stacks everything.

## Holdings

**Holdings** lists every open position: price, quantity, average cost, value, a 24-hour change, an allocation bar and profit or loss. Use the search box to filter by asset or name.

![The Holdings table](/images/portfolio/holdings.png)

- Double-click a row to open the asset's detail sheet with its figures and its own transactions.
![An asset's detail sheet with its figures and transactions](/images/portfolio/asset-detail.png)

- Use a row's menu for **View Transactions** or **Remap Asset…**. Remap moves an asset's transactions to a different market.

## Portfolio cards on the chart grid

You can put portfolio cards next to your charts. Open **Add Chart**, choose the **Portfolio** section, then set **Which portfolio** and **What to show**: **Portfolio Value Chart**, **Portfolio Value**, **Allocation** or **Top Performers**.

![Allocation cards at four sizes](/images/portfolio/card-allocation.png)

Cards have no candles, alerts, drawings or scripts. To change the portfolio a card shows, remove it and add it again.
