# Language

DegenView's interface is available in these languages:

- English
- Deutsch (German)
- Español (Spanish)
- Русский (Russian)
- 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)

By default the app follows your computer's language. If DegenView does not have it, the app shows English.

## How to switch language

1. Open **Settings** with ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux).
2. Choose **Appearance**.
3. Under **Language**, open the **Interface language** list and pick a language. Each one is listed by its own name. **Same as the system** goes back to following your computer.
4. DegenView asks **Restart to change the language?** Pick **Restart Now** to close and reopen the app, or **Later** to keep the choice for the next start.

The new language applies after the restart.

## Finding the setting in another language

If the app is in a language you cannot read, open Settings with the shortcut above and look for these names:

| App language | Settings page | Section | List |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | Appearance | Language | Interface language |
| Deutsch | Erscheinungsbild | Sprache | Oberflächensprache |
| Español | Apariencia | Idioma | Idioma de la interfaz |
| Русский | Вид | Язык | Язык интерфейса |
| 简体中文 | 外观 | 语言 | 界面语言 |

## What stays as it is

Symbols and tickers, market data from providers, Pine Script code and its errors, and names you gave to things such as layouts and watchlists are not translated.

## Good to know

- Regional variants use the closest language: a computer set to Mexican Spanish gets Spanish, and one set to Austrian German gets German.
- Chinese is Simplified only. A computer set to Traditional Chinese gets English.
- Chinese needs a Chinese font on your system. macOS and Windows have one. On Linux, if you see empty boxes instead of characters, install one such as Noto Sans CJK.
- The language is saved on this computer only.
