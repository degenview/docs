# Appearance

DegenView offers three themes:

- **Dark** — the default.
- **Light** — a bright theme.
- **System** — follow your OS light/dark setting.

Change the theme from Settings ▸ Appearance, or from the appearance controls if your window
shows them.

## Chart styling

Beyond the theme, chart appearance — colors, candles, and style — can be customized
per chart. These settings are part of what a [saved view](/workspace/saved-views) remembers.
