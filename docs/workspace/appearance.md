# Appearance and themes

DegenView can look dark or light, or follow your computer. The choice applies to every window.

![The Appearance page in Settings](/images/settings/appearance.png)

## How to change the theme

1. Open **Settings** with ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux).
2. Choose **Appearance**.
3. Under **Theme**, pick **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.

**Dark** is the default. **System** follows your computer's light or dark setting and changes when it does.

## Chart colours are separate

Your own chart colours do not depend on the theme. If you pick a colour for rising candles, it stays that colour in both themes. A colour you never set follows the theme. Chart colours, backgrounds and grid lines are set per chart. See [Chart settings and templates](/charts/settings-templates).

## Tab titles

The **Tabs** setting on the same page chooses what a tab shows: **Symbol, price and layout**, **Symbol only** or **Layout name only**. **Restore default** appears once you change it. See [Tabs and windows](/workspace/tabs-windows).

::: info Newer builds
The **Tabs** setting is newer than the last release candidate, so it may be missing from your build.
:::

## The Pine editor has its own themes

Code in Pine Studio uses an editor theme, which you choose in **Settings ▸ Pine Studio**. The default, Automatic, follows the app theme, and you can create your own. See [Writing scripts](/pine/writing-scripts).
